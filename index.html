Estimate for a new patient

How it is calculated

  1. Patients ahead – waiting patients in the same department ranked higher (priority score + ageing).
  2. Consultation duration – average minutes per department (e.g. Cardiology 15, Dermatology 8).
  3. Doctor availability – available doctors share the load; if none is free, add half a consultation for the one in progress.
  4. Formula: if ahead < free doctors → 0 min, else (ahead − free + 1) ÷ doctors × duration (+ ½ duration if none free). No doctor on duty → ahead × duration + 30.
  5. Emergency patients rank first, so they have fewer patients ahead.

      The problem

      Long, unpredictable waits frustrate patients, crowd waiting rooms, and delay urgent care.

      The solution

      A queue system that ranks patients by urgency and arrival, estimates waits, and lets staff balance doctor workloads.

      Key benefits

      • Transparent waiting times
      • Urgent cases seen sooner
      • Better doctor utilisation
      • Data for planning staff

      Future improvements

      • Secure backend, authentication & role-based access
      • Privacy protection and audit logs
      • SMS/WhatsApp notifications
      • Integration with clinically validated triage tools
